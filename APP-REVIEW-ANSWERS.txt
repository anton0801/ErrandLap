3. FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Errand Run is an offline planner for everyday errands: pharmacy, post office, bank, clinic, shops, paperwork. Audience: adults running household errands; general audience, no age-restricted content.
A to-do list does not know the post office closes at 14:00 and is 20 minutes away, and a map app does not know the user has only 90 free minutes. The app answers "what can I actually finish today". The user enters errands, the places they happen at (opening hours per weekday, lunch breaks, last entry) and their free windows; it builds a route that fits, counting travel, time inside each place and a parking buffer. What does not fit moves to the next window that works. It never predicts queue length: it uses the user's estimate, records the real duration, then plans with that average.

4. SETUP AND MAIN FEATURES
NO LOGIN REQUIRED. No accounts, so no demo credentials exist or are needed, and no sample files. Works right after install. About a minute from a fresh install:
1) Onboarding: "Next" x3, then "Set Up and Start".
2) Setup: any home address and a travel mode, "Save Setup". Only these two are required.
3) Places tab > "Add a Place": name, address, opening hours, save.
4) Errands tab > "Add an Errand": title, that place, duration, save.
5) Settings (gear, top right of Today) > Windows: add a window with a start and end.
6) Today tab: the route is built and every stop explained; "Why This Order" explains the ordering, starting the run opens live mode.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None, other than Apple frameworks on the device:
- MapKit / CoreLocation geocoding (MKDirections, CLGeocoder), used only to turn a typed address into a coordinate and get travel time between two points. Optional: if unavailable, travel times can be typed by hand and all else works.
- Local notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter), scheduled on device.
- System photo picker (PhotosUI), optional, needs no library access.
NO backend server, NO accounts, NO analytics, NO advertising, NO tracking, NO third-party SDK, NO payment processor, NO subscription, NO AI service, NO external data provider. The app makes no network requests of its own; all data stays in its container on the device, and it contains no CLLocationManager.
